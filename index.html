What can help

Six ways to make speaking easier

01

Create a safe classroom

Treat mistakes as a normal part of learning. Agree on respectful listening, avoid public comparisons, and let students prepare before speaking.

02

Build up gradually

Move from private rehearsal to pairs, small groups, and whole-class speaking. Keep each next step achievable.

03

Use discipline-related topics

Discuss familiar tools, cases, processes, and workplace situations students already understand.

04

Rehearse with digital tools

Record short voice notes and try again. Apps and conversational AI can provide low-pressure practice at a learner's own pace.

05

Give focused feedback

Recognize what worked, then choose one or two next steps. Avoid correcting every error during fluency practice.

06

Practice self-regulation

Pause for steady breaths, set a small goal, and use supportive self-talk. The aim is to share a message, not speak perfectly.

STEP 01 - 2 MIN

Get ready

Choose a familiar topic and note three key words.

STEP 02 - 3 MIN

Speak aloud

Record a short answer. The first take is just rehearsal.

STEP 03 - 5 MIN

Talk in pairs

Share your idea and ask a follow-up question.

STEP 04 - 1 MIN

Notice progress
